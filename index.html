Platform Specifications

More Information

Comprehensive breakdown of features, live data feeds, and calculation output specs.

🌐 Real-Time Data Engine

CurrenX connects to institutional-grade forex rate APIs, providing synchronized rates for over 43 global fiat currencies. Rates are continuously synced in the background every 30 minutes.

⚡ Output & Features Summary

  • Interactive Split-Flap Display: Dynamic character animation rendering exact conversion results.
  • Multi-Currency Rate Matrix: Compare 1 base currency against USD, EUR, GBP, INR, JPY, AUD, CAD, and CHF simultaneously.
  • Offline History Caching: Save and manage your recent conversion logs in browser local storage.
  • 6 Custom Color Themes: Switch between Dark 🌙, Light ☀️, Pink 🌸, Emerald 🌲, Cyberpunk 💜, and Sunset 🌅 modes.

Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about exchange rates, updates, and usage.

How often are currency exchange rates updated? ▾
Exchange rates are fetched live from open.er-api.com and automatically refreshed in the background every 30 minutes to ensure high accuracy.
Is CurrenX free to use? ▾
Yes! CurrenX is 100% free with no registration required for basic conversions, though signing in unlocks saved preferences and history persistence across devices.
How does the Multi-Currency Compare feature work? ▾
The Compare matrix allows you to select a base currency and amount, instantly calculating the converted values for 8 major world currencies simultaneously.
Are my conversion history logs secure? ▾
All conversion logs and theme settings are stored locally in your web browser's localStorage, keeping your information private and secure.

Platform Overview

About CurrenX

Learn about the platform mission, data engine, and visual exchange technology.

🚀 Mission & Vision

CurrenX was built to deliver a modern, visually captivating, and ultra-responsive currency conversion experience. By combining live institutional data feeds with split-flap board mechanics, multi-currency matrix comparison, and custom theme engine, CurrenX sets a new standard in web financial tools.

🛠 Technology Stack

Constructed with zero heavy external framework dependencies using HTML5, Vanilla CSS3 Custom Properties, JavaScript ES6+, Open Exchange Rates API, and LocalStorage caching.
